Quiet on the outside.

The camera, speakers and wiring hide behind the mat. The window sits where the screen really is on the board (right of centre, low), so the frame stays as small as the kit allows. The landscape-and-clock image is a mockup, not running firmware.

Serviceable on the inside.

Four M3 screws secure the back. The USB plug sits in the bottom band and the cable leaves through the back; the plinth has a matching notch. Vent slots let heat and some speaker sound escape. Rear seats use removable insulating foam tape; no PCB holes are assumed.

Print, check, then assemble.

Use a 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers, four walls and 15–20% infill. The STLs are already bed-oriented. Check the paper template at 100% scale and the thin gauge before a full print. See assembly notes and dimension sources.
